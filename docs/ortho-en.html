Dental Equipment and Tools Section

We use the latest devices and technologies to ensure treatment accuracy, fast results, and the highest standards of comfort and safety.

Digital Oral Scanner

The latest digital imaging technology to create accurate 3D models of teeth and gums, which facilitates diagnosis and provides a comfortable experience for the patient compared to traditional methods.

Surgical Implant Motor

A specialized device that provides high precision during dental implant procedures, ensuring implant stability with the highest safety standards for patients.

Clear Aligners (Invisible Braces)

An innovative technique for teeth correction without metal wires, made from comfortable and effective transparent materials, providing a natural look during treatment.

Panoramic, Cephalometric, and CBCT X-rays

Advanced X-ray devices for accurate diagnosis, including panoramic images of teeth and jaws, cephalometric images for planning orthodontic treatments, in addition to 3D CBCT technology for more diagnostic detail.
